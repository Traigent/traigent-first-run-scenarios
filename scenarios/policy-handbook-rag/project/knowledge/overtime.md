# Overtime

## Approval

Overtime must be approved by your line manager before it is worked.
Overtime worked without prior approval is not paid and does not earn time
off in lieu.

## Overtime pay for hourly staff

Grades 1 to 4 are paid hourly, and grade 5 and above are salaried. Hourly
staff are paid overtime at 1.5 times their hourly rate for hours worked
beyond 40 in a week. Hours worked on a Sunday or on a public holiday
are paid at 2 times the hourly rate whatever the weekly total. An employee
rostered on a public holiday also receives a day in lieu under the Public
Holidays policy.

## Time off in lieu for salaried staff

Salaried staff do not receive overtime pay. Instead they receive time off in
lieu, hour for hour, for approved additional hours. Time off in lieu must be
taken within 2 months of being earned or it is lost.

## Weekly cap

No employee may work more than 12 hours of overtime in a week without the
approval of the depot manager. The cap exists to protect rest periods and
is applied strictly.

## Drivers

Overtime for drivers never extends driving time beyond 9 hours in a day, and
the daily and weekly rest rules in the driver handbook continue to apply.
Loading and paperwork count as working time but not as driving time.

## Claiming overtime

Overtime is claimed on your timesheet, which must be submitted by noon on
Monday for the previous week. Approved overtime is paid in the next payroll
run. Late timesheets are paid in the following payroll run.

## Rest breaks

Anyone working more than 6 hours in a day takes an unpaid break of at least
30 minutes. Overtime does not remove the right to the break.
