# Annual leave

Marrowfield Logistics gives every full-time employee 25 days of annual leave
per year. Part-time employees receive the same entitlement pro rata to their
contracted hours. The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

Leave accrues monthly at one twelfth of the annual entitlement, so an
employee who joins mid-year receives the share of the year that remains.
After five complete years of service the entitlement rises to 27 days from
the start of the next leave year.

## Carrying leave over

Up to 5 days of unused leave can be carried into the following leave year.
Carried-over days must be used by 31 March of that year or they are lost.
Leave above the 5-day limit cannot be carried over, and leave cannot be
exchanged for cash while you remain employed.

## Booking leave

All leave is requested through the People Portal and approved by your line
manager. The notice you must give depends on the length of the request:
- a request for 3 or more consecutive days needs at least 10 working days'
  notice;
- a request for 1 or 2 days needs at least 2 working days' notice.

No single period of leave may exceed 10 consecutive working days without the
approval of your department head.

The first two full weeks of December are peak weeks for depot operations.
Any leave request that falls inside the peak weeks needs the approval of the
depot manager in addition to your line manager, and it may be declined on
operational grounds.

## Leaving the company

When you leave, any accrued leave you have not taken is paid in your final
salary at your base rate of pay. If you have taken more leave than you have
accrued at your leaving date, the excess is deducted from your final salary.

## Sickness during leave

If you fall ill during a period of annual leave, the days covered by a fit
note can be reclaimed and taken as sick leave instead. The Sick Leave policy
explains the notification steps and the deadline for reclaiming the days.

## Questions

Questions about your leave balance go to your line manager first. The People
team can correct a balance that the People Portal shows incorrectly.
