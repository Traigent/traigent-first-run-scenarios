# Health and safety on site

## Protective equipment

A hi-vis vest and safety boots must be worn at all times in the yard and the
warehouse. Hard hats must be worn in the zones marked with hard-hat signs.
Protective equipment is issued free of charge and replaced when worn.

## Forklift trucks

Only employees holding a valid in-house forklift certificate may drive a
forklift truck. The certificate is renewed every 3 years through a refresher
course arranged by the shift supervisor.

## Vehicles and pedestrians

The speed limit in the yard is 10 mph. Pedestrians keep to the marked
walkways and cross vehicle routes only at the marked crossing points.

## Manual handling

Nothing over 25 kg is lifted by one person. Use lifting equipment or ask a
colleague to help. Manual handling training is part of the health and safety
module every new starter completes.

## Near misses

A near miss is an event that could have caused injury but did not. Record
every near miss in the Safety Log before the end of your shift. Near misses
are reviewed weekly and are never used to blame the reporter.

## Injuries

Report any injury immediately to the shift supervisor, however minor. The
supervisor arranges first aid and makes sure an entry is made in the Safety
Log within 24 hours of the injury.

## First aiders

Every shift has at least 1 trained first aider for every 25 staff on duty.
The names of the first aiders on each shift are shown on the notice boards
and in the Safety Log.

## Fire

Fire drills are held every quarter. When the alarm sounds, leave by the
nearest exit, do not use the lifts, and go to the assembly point in the
north car park. Do not return to the building until the fire warden says so.

## Spillages

Cordon off any spillage and report it to the shift supervisor. Chemical
spillages are cleared only by staff trained in the spill procedure.
