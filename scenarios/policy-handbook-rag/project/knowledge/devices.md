# Company devices

## Standard issue

Every employee who needs one is issued a laptop, which is replaced every 4
years. Roles that need a phone are issued a company phone, replaced every 3
years. Drivers and yard staff are issued a ruggedised handheld scanner in
place of a laptop. Devices remain company property.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen device to the IT Service Desk within 24 hours so it
can be wiped remotely. Theft must also be reported to the police and the
crime reference number given to the IT Service Desk. If the device held
Confidential or Restricted data, the one-hour incident reporting rule in the
Security policy applies as well.

## Faults and repairs

Report a faulty device through the IT Service Desk portal. If the fault
cannot be fixed the same day, a loan device is provided within 2 working
days. Do not attempt repairs yourself or use an outside repairer.

## Accessories

Each employee may order accessories such as a headset, mouse, keyboard or
bag up to £100 per year through the IT Service Desk. Accessories are ordered
by the IT Service Desk, so no receipt or expense claim is needed.

## Personal use and software

Reasonable personal use of a company device is allowed outside working
hours. Personal software is not installed on company devices, and
administrator rights are not granted to employees. Software for business
use is requested through the IT Service Desk.

## Personal devices

Company email may be used on a personal phone only if the phone is enrolled
in the company's mobile device management service. Personal laptops and
tablets may never be used for company work or company email.

## Leaving the company

Return every company device and accessory within 5 working days of your last
day. The replacement value of any device not returned is deducted from your
final salary.

## Home working equipment

Monitors, desks and chairs for a home workspace are covered by the Remote
Working policy rather than by this policy.
