# Speaking up

## What to report

Marrowfield Logistics wants to hear about wrongdoing so that it can be put
right. Report any concern about fraud, bribery, a danger to health or safety,
damage to the environment, a breach of the law or of company policy, or an
attempt to cover any of these up.

Personal grievances about your own employment, such as a disagreement with
your manager about your role, are not handled under this policy. Raise those
through the grievance procedure instead.

## How to report

You may raise a concern with any of the following:
- your line manager;
- the Ethics Officer;
- the Speak Up line, which is run by an independent provider and is
  available 24 hours a day.

Reports to the Speak Up line may be made anonymously. An anonymous report is
investigated in the same way, although the company cannot update an
anonymous reporter on progress.

## What happens next

Every report is acknowledged within 5 working days. The Ethics Officer
appoints an investigator, and the outcome of the investigation is given to
the reporter within 30 working days. Where an investigation needs longer, the
reporter is told in writing why and when to expect the outcome.

## Protection for reporters

The identity of a reporter is kept confidential and is shared only with the
people who need it to investigate. Retaliation against anyone who raises a
concern in good faith is a disciplinary offence and can lead to dismissal.

Employees may also report concerns to an external regulator, and the same
protection applies.

## Malicious reports

A report made in bad faith, knowing it to be untrue, is itself a
disciplinary matter. A report that turns out to be mistaken but was made in
good faith is never penalised.

## Records

Case files are kept for 6 years after the case is closed, as set out in the
Records and Data Retention policy.
