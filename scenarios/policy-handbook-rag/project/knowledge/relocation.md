# Relocation

## Company-requested moves

When the company asks you to move to a site more than 50 miles from your
current home, you are entitled to a relocation allowance of up to £6,000.
Moves of 50 miles or less, and moves you request yourself, do not qualify
for the allowance.

The allowance covers:
- a removal firm;
- temporary accommodation for up to 8 weeks;
- one house-hunting trip, including travel and up to 2 nights' accommodation;
- legal fees on the sale of your old home and the purchase of the new one.

Company-requested moves also come with 3 days of paid moving leave.

## Claiming

Relocation claims are submitted through the Expense Hub using the relocation
code within 6 months of your move date. Claims are approved by the People
team, not through the usual expense approval limits, because the People team
holds the relocation agreement.

## Repayment

If you leave the company within 12 months of the move you repay 100% of the
allowance you received. If you leave between 12 and 24 months after the move
you repay 50%. Nothing is repayable after 24 months.

## Voluntary transfers

If you apply for and accept a role at another site, the move is a voluntary
transfer. Voluntary transfers do not receive the relocation allowance but do
receive 2 days of paid moving leave.

## Where to start

Relocation is agreed with the People team before the move, using the offer
letter or transfer letter as the record of the request. Speak to the People
team as soon as a move is proposed so that the agreement is in place before
you incur any cost.
