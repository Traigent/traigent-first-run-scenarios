# Remote and hybrid working

## Who can work remotely

Office-based roles are hybrid: you must work at least 3 days per week from
your contracted site. Operational roles, meaning drivers, warehouse
operatives and yard staff, are not eligible for remote working because the
work cannot be done away from the site.

Fully remote working is available only with your department head's approval
and a written remote working agreement. Each agreement is reviewed every 12
months.

## Core hours

Wherever you work, you must be available during the core hours of 10:00 to
15:00. Hours outside the core hours are agreed with your line manager.

## Home working allowance

Employees who work from home on 2 or more days per week receive a home
working allowance of £25 per month, paid through payroll. The allowance
starts from the first full month of qualifying home working.

## Home equipment

You may claim up to £250 once every 5 years towards a desk, chair or monitor
for your home workspace. The claim is made through the Expense Hub with
receipts. Laptops and phones are covered by the Devices policy, not by this
allowance.

## Workspace assessment

Complete the home workspace assessment in the People Portal within your first
month of working from home, and again whenever your home workspace changes.

## Working abroad

You may work from abroad for up to 20 working days per calendar year. Working
abroad needs the approval of the People team, requested at least 4 weeks in
advance, and is allowed only from countries on the approved country list.
Stays longer than 20 working days are not permitted for tax and insurance
reasons.

## Security while remote

The Information Security policy applies wherever you work. In particular,
use the VPN on any network you do not control and keep Confidential and
Restricted information off shared household devices.
