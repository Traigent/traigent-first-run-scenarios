# Parental leave

## Maternity and adoption leave

Employees who are having a baby or adopting a child may take up to 52 weeks
of leave. The first 16 weeks of leave are paid at full pay. Weeks 17 to 39
are paid at the statutory rate, and any leave after week 39 is unpaid.

To qualify for the enhanced full-pay period you must have completed 26
weeks of continuous service by the 15th week before the expected week of
birth or, for adoption, by the week you are matched with a child. Employees
with shorter service receive statutory pay only.

Tell the People team in writing by the 15th week before the expected week
of birth, giving the expected date and the date you want your leave to
start, together with the certificate issued by your midwife or doctor.
Adoption leave is notified within 7 days of being matched.

## Partner leave

The partner of a person giving birth or adopting may take 4 weeks of partner
leave at full pay. It must be taken within 12 months of the birth or
placement, either as one block of 4 weeks or as two blocks of 2 weeks.
Partners may also attend 2 antenatal or adoption appointments on paid time.

## Keeping in touch

While on maternity or adoption leave you may work up to 10 keeping-in-touch
days without ending your leave. Each day is paid at your normal daily rate.
The days are voluntary and are agreed with your line manager in advance.

## Returning to work

If your leave lasts 26 weeks or less you return to the same job. If it lasts
longer than 26 weeks you return to the same job where that is practicable,
and otherwise to a suitable alternative role on terms no less favourable.

If you do not return to work for at least 3 months after your leave ends, the
enhanced part of your pay (the amount above the statutory rate) becomes
repayable. Statutory pay is never repayable.

## Antenatal appointments

Pregnant employees may attend antenatal appointments on paid time without
limit. Show your line manager the appointment card if asked.

## Shared parental leave

Parents may share the leave and pay described above between them. Ask the
People team for the shared leave form at least 8 weeks before the leave you
want to share is due to start.
