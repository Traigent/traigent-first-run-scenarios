# Visitors and contractors

## Booking a visitor

Book every visitor in the visitor system at least 1 working day before the
visit. A group of more than 12 visitors needs 5 working days' notice and the
site manager's agreement.

## Arrival

Visitors sign in at reception, receive a visitor badge and wear it visibly
throughout their visit. They sign out and return the badge when they leave.
Visitor parking is in the south car park.

## Escorting

The host is responsible for their visitors at all times. In operational
areas, meaning the yard, the warehouse and the loading bays, visitors must be
escorted by their host at all times. In office areas a visitor may walk
between reception and the meeting rooms unescorted once signed in.

## Protective equipment

Visitors entering the yard or the warehouse collect a hi-vis vest from
reception and must wear closed shoes. Visitors without suitable footwear are
not admitted to operational areas.

## Contractors

Contractors must complete the site induction before starting work on site.
The induction is valid for 12 months. Hot work, such as welding or grinding,
needs a permit to work issued by the site manager before it starts.

## Photography

Photography and filming on site require the site manager's permission.

## Visiting drivers

Drivers who are not employed by the company wait in the driver waiting room
while their vehicle is loaded or unloaded, and do not enter the warehouse.

## Security

Visitors are never given access to company systems and must not connect
their own devices to the company network. The Information Security policy
applies to everything a visitor sees or handles on site.
