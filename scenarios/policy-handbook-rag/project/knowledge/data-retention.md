# Records and data retention

## How long records are kept

Records are kept for the periods below and then deleted or destroyed:
- customer delivery records: 7 years after the delivery;
- CCTV recordings: 30 days, unless preserved for an incident;
- recruitment records of unsuccessful candidates: 6 months after the
  decision;
- employee records: 6 years after employment ends;
- whistleblowing case files: 6 years after the case is closed;
- email: archived after 2 years and deleted after 5 years.

## Legal hold

The Legal team may place records under a legal hold when litigation or a
regulatory investigation is expected. Records under a legal hold are not
deleted, even when their retention period has expired, until the Legal team
lifts the hold.

## Deleting records

Records are deleted by the Records team according to the schedule above. Do
not delete company records yourself, and do not keep personal copies of
records past their retention period.

## Requests from individuals

A request from a person for a copy of the personal data the company holds
about them is called a subject access request. Pass any such request to the
Records team on the day you receive it. The company answers within 1 month
of receiving the request; for complex requests the Records team may extend
this by a further 2 months and tells the person why.

## Data Protection Officer

The Data Protection Officer is contacted through the Records team and
advises on any question about personal data, including whether a record can
be shared outside the company.

## Personal data in everyday work

Collect only the personal data a task needs, store it in the company systems
built for it, and do not keep it in personal folders or spreadsheets. The
Information Security policy sets out how classified information is handled.
