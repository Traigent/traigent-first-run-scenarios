# Company vehicles

## Daily checks

Before the first journey of the day, complete the walk-round check in the
Fleet App. Any defect is reported in the Fleet App before the vehicle is
driven, and a vehicle with a safety-critical defect is not driven until the
Fleet team clears it.

## Accidents

Report any accident or damage to the Fleet team within 24 hours. Complete
the accident form kept in the vehicle document wallet and photograph the
scene. Never admit liability at the scene; exchange details and let the
Fleet team handle the claim.

## Fines

Parking fines, speeding fines and other penalties incurred while driving a
company vehicle are the driver's responsibility. Where a fine is issued to
the company and reassigned to the driver, an administration fee of £25 is
added.

## Private use

Vans and trucks may not be used privately. Pool cars are for business use
only and are booked in the Fleet App; keys are collected from reception and
returned there with the fuel at the level recorded on collection.

## Fuel cards

A fuel card may be used only to fuel the vehicle it is assigned to. Never
share the card's PIN. Keep fuel receipts for 3 months in case the Fleet team
asks for them.

## Licence checks

The Fleet team checks every driver's licence every 6 months. You must tell
the Fleet team about any new penalty points or motoring conviction within 7
days.

Drivers with 6 or more penalty points have a driving review with the Fleet
team. Drivers with 9 or more penalty points are removed from driving duties
until the points fall below 9.

## Mobile phones

Phones may be used in a vehicle only through a hands-free system. Handheld
use while driving is a disciplinary matter.

## Speed and safety on site

The yard speed limit and the pedestrian rules in the Safety policy apply to
every vehicle on site, including visiting vehicles.
