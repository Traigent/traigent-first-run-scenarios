# Starting at Marrowfield Logistics

## Your first day

Bring your identity and right-to-work documents on your first day; you cannot
be paid until the People team has checked them. Collect your laptop or
handheld from the IT Service Desk. Company induction sessions run on the
first Monday of each month, and every new starter attends the next one after
their start date.

## Your buddy

Every new starter is assigned a buddy during their first week. Your buddy is
an experienced colleague outside your reporting line who can answer everyday
questions about how the company works.

## Probation

All roles have a probation period of 6 months. Your line manager holds a
probation review with you at 3 months and again at the end of probation.
Probation may be extended once, by up to 3 months, where more time is needed
to reach the required standard.

During probation the notice period is 1 week on either side. After probation
the notice period is 1 month for grades 1 to 4 and 3 months for grade 5 and
above.

## Mandatory training

Complete the following training within your first 30 days:
- security awareness;
- health and safety;
- data protection.

Role-specific training, such as the forklift certificate, is arranged by your
line manager and has its own timescale.

## Benefits enrolment

Enrol in benefits within 30 days of your start date. After that window,
changes can be made only at the annual enrolment in November or within 30
days of a life event, as described in the Benefits policy. Pension enrolment
happens automatically from your first pay.

## Systems access

Access to company systems is set up before your first day and activated once
you have completed the security awareness module, which is available on your
first morning.
