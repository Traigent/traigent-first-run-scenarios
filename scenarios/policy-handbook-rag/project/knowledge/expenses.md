# Expenses

Business expenses are claimed through the Expense Hub. Submit each claim
within 30 days of the date of the spend. A claim submitted more than 30 days
after the spend needs your department head's approval, and a claim more than
90 days old is not paid.

## Receipts

Attach a receipt for any single item over £15. Meal receipts must be
itemised; a card slip alone is not accepted. Where a receipt is lost, add a
note explaining the spend and your line manager decides whether to allow it.

## Meals and overnight stays

When you stay away overnight on business you may claim meals up to £35 per
day in the United Kingdom and up to £60 per day abroad. The allowance is a
limit on actual spend, not a flat payment. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## Mileage

Business travel in your own car is paid at 45 pence per mile for the first
10,000 business miles in a tax year and 25 pence per mile after that.
Business travel by bicycle is paid at 20 pence per mile. Mileage is measured
from your normal place of work or from home, whichever is shorter.

## Approval limits

Who approves a claim depends on its total value:
- claims up to £500 are approved by your line manager;
- claims over £500 and up to £5,000 are approved by your department head;
- claims over £5,000 are approved by the finance director.

Relocation claims follow the Relocation policy and are approved by the
People team rather than through these limits.

## Payment

Approved claims are paid in the next payroll run after approval. Payroll runs
on the 25th of each month, so a claim approved on the 26th is paid the
following month.

## Company cards

Holders of a company card must reconcile every transaction in the Expense
Hub within 14 days of the statement date. Personal spending on a company
card is not allowed and is recovered from salary.

## Not reimbursed

The following are never reimbursed: alcohol, parking or traffic fines,
personal entertainment, class upgrades beyond the Travel policy, and the
costs of a partner or family member accompanying you.
