# Business travel

All business travel is booked through the Travel Desk. Flights should be
booked at least 14 days before departure wherever the trip is known that far
in advance; late bookings must state the reason.

## Approval

Domestic travel needs your line manager's approval. International travel
needs your department head's approval before the Travel Desk will book it.

## Flights

Flights of under 6 hours are booked in economy class. Flights of 6 hours or
more may be booked in premium economy. Business class is not booked without
the written approval of the chief executive.

## Rail

Rail travel is booked in standard class. First class may be booked only when
it is cheaper than standard class at the time of booking.

## Hotels

Hotel rooms are booked up to £140 per night in the United Kingdom and up to
£190 per night abroad. Breakfast is included where the hotel offers it.
Stays above the limit need the reason recorded on the booking and your
department head's approval.

## Taxis and local travel

Use public transport where it is practical. Taxis may be used when public
transport is impractical, when you are carrying equipment, or for journeys
starting after 22:00. Taxi receipts are attached to your expense claim.

## Insurance

The company travel insurance policy covers every employee on business travel
automatically. Carry the insurance card issued by the Travel Desk, which
lists the emergency assistance number.

## Adding personal days

You may add up to 3 personal days to a business trip. Any extra cost of
flights, hotels or other travel arising from the personal days is paid by
you, and the company insurance does not cover the personal days.

## Visas and passports

Visa fees for business travel are reimbursed through expenses. Passport fees
are not reimbursed. Apply for visas early, as the Travel Desk cannot speed
up a consulate.

## Loyalty schemes

Air miles and hotel loyalty points earned on business travel may be kept by
the traveller, but they must never influence the choice of a more expensive
booking.

## Meals while travelling

Meals on business trips are claimed under the Expenses policy, which sets
the daily limits.
