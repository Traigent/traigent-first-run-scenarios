# Sick leave and sick pay

If you are too ill to work, telephone your line manager before 09:00 on each
day of absence. Shift staff at the depots must call at least 1 hour before
the start of their shift. A phone call is expected; a message is acceptable
only when you are genuinely unable to speak.

## Certifying an absence

You may self-certify an absence of up to 5 working days. From the sixth
working day of absence you must provide a fit note from a doctor, and a new
fit note must cover every further period of absence.

## Company sick pay

Once you have completed probation, Marrowfield Logistics pays company sick
pay as follows in any rolling 12-month period:
- the first 20 working days of sickness are paid at full pay;
- the next 40 working days are paid at half pay;
- after that, only statutory sick pay is paid.

During probation, only statutory sick pay is paid. Company sick pay is paid
only where the notification and certification rules above have been
followed.

## Returning to work

After any absence longer than 3 working days your line manager will hold a
return-to-work conversation with you on your first day back. Its purpose is
to check that you are fit to return and to agree any adjustments.

## Frequent absence

Three separate absences in any 6-month period trigger an attendance review
meeting with your line manager and the People team. The meeting looks for
underlying causes and agrees support; it is not a disciplinary meeting.

## Medical appointments

Routine appointments should be booked outside working hours where possible.
Where an appointment cannot be moved, up to 2 hours of paid time is allowed
for it. Longer appointments are taken as annual leave or unpaid time.

## Sickness during annual leave

If you fall ill while on annual leave, the days covered by a fit note can be
reclaimed as sick leave. Submit the fit note to the People team within 5
working days of returning to work; the leave days are then credited back to
your balance and the period is treated as sick leave under the rules above.
