# Training and development

## Personal training budget

Every employee has a personal training budget of £800 per year for external
courses, conferences and certifications. Requests are made through the
Learning Portal and approved by your line manager. Unused budget does not
carry over to the next year.

Internal courses run by the People team are free and do not count against
your budget.

## Professional memberships

The company pays professional membership fees of up to £200 per year. This
is in addition to the £800 training budget, not part of it.

## Sponsored qualifications

A qualification costing more than £800 may be sponsored by the company. It
needs your department head's sign-off and a signed repayment agreement. Under
the agreement, if you leave within 12 months of completing the qualification
you repay 100% of the cost, and if you leave between 12 and 24 months after
completing it you repay 50%. Nothing is repayable after 24 months.

Employees studying for a sponsored qualification receive 5 paid study days
per year. The day of each exam is paid in addition to the study days.

## Conferences

Conference fees come from your training budget. Travel and accommodation for
a conference are booked and paid under the Travel policy and do not come out
of your training budget.

## Ordering training

Any external training over £250 needs a purchase order under the Procurement
policy. Raise the purchase order through the Learning Portal once your line
manager has approved the request.

## Records

Certificates for completed training are uploaded to the Learning Portal,
which holds your training record. Line managers review the record at each
annual review.
