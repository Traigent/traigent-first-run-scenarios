# Information security

## Passwords and sign-in

Passwords on company systems must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor
authentication is required on every company system, with no exceptions.
The company password manager is provided for every employee and is the only
approved place to store passwords.

## Locking screens and desks

Company devices lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Lock your
screen manually whenever you leave your desk. Papers classed as Confidential
or Restricted must be locked away overnight and must not be left on desks or
printers.

## Data classification

Information at Marrowfield Logistics is classified as Public, Internal,
Confidential or Restricted. Restricted information may be held only on
company-managed encrypted devices and never on removable media. USB storage
devices are blocked on company laptops. Confidential and Restricted
information is not sent to personal email accounts.

## Phishing

Report a suspicious email using the Report Phish button in the mail client.
Do not forward the email to colleagues, and do not open its attachments or
links. The Security Operations team replies to every report.

## Security incidents

A security incident is any loss, theft or suspected compromise of company
data or of a device holding it. Report a security incident within 1 hour of
discovering it to the Security Operations team using the 24-hour security
line. Reporting quickly is never penalised; failing to report is.

## Training

Security awareness training must be completed within 30 days of joining and
then every 12 months. Access to company systems is suspended for anyone whose
training is more than 30 days overdue.

## Working away from the office

Use the company VPN whenever you connect from a public network. Use the
privacy screen supplied with your laptop when working in public places, and
never leave a device unattended in a vehicle or a public place.

## Visitors

Visitors must be escorted in operational areas at all times, and must never
be given access to a company system. The Visitors policy sets out the
sign-in and escort rules in full.
