# Benefits

## Pension

You are enrolled in the company pension from your first pay. You contribute
at least 3% of your salary and the company contributes 6%. If you choose to
contribute 5% or more, the company contribution rises to 8%. You may opt out
through the People Portal, but the company contribution is lost while you are
opted out.

## Life assurance

Every employee is covered by life assurance of 4 times base salary from their
first day. Nominate a beneficiary in the People Portal.

## Private medical insurance

Private medical insurance is available to employees who have completed
probation. Cover for your partner and children can be added at your own cost
through payroll.

## Employee assistance programme

The employee assistance programme offers confidential support 24 hours a day
on personal, financial and legal matters. It is available to you and to
members of your household.

## Gym and cycling

The company pays a gym subsidy of £20 per month towards any gym membership.
The cycle-to-work scheme allows up to £1,500 of salary sacrifice for a
bicycle and safety equipment.

## Eye tests

Employees who use a screen for their work may claim an eye test every 2
years, up to £25, and up to £60 towards glasses prescribed for screen use.
Claim through the Expense Hub with the optician's receipt.

## Long service awards

Employees receive a £250 voucher at 10 years of service and a £500 voucher at
20 years of service.

## Changing your benefits

Benefit choices can be changed at the annual enrolment window in November or
within 30 days of a life event such as marriage, the birth or adoption of a
child, or a change in your partner's cover. Outside those windows the choices
you have made stay in place.
