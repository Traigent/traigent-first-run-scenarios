# Procurement

## Purchase orders

Any purchase over £250 needs a purchase order raised before the order is
placed. Suppliers are told to quote the purchase order number on their
invoice, and an invoice without a valid purchase order number may not be
paid. Purchases of £250 or less can be made without a purchase order using
a company card or an expense claim.

## Quotes and tenders

Purchases over £1,000 need 3 written quotes. Purchases over £25,000 go
through a formal tender run by the Procurement team. Where only one supplier
can provide what is needed, complete a single-source justification form,
signed by your department head, instead of the quotes.

## New suppliers

A supplier that has not worked with the company before must be vetted by the
Procurement team before a purchase order can be raised. Vetting takes up to
10 working days, so allow for it when planning a purchase. The preferred
supplier list on the intranet shows suppliers already vetted.

## Software

Any software purchase or subscription needs the approval of the IT Service
Desk, whatever its value, so that licences and security are checked.

## Contracts

Contracts with suppliers are signed only by a department head or above. No
employee may make a verbal commitment to a supplier on the company's
behalf.

## Paying suppliers

Valid invoices are paid 30 days from the date they are received. Invoices
are sent to the accounts payable mailbox, not to individual employees.

## Personal purchases

Company accounts, company cards and supplier discounts may not be used for
personal purchases.

## Training purchases

External training is ordered under this policy through the Learning Portal,
as the Training and Development policy describes.
