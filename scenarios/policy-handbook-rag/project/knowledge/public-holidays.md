# Public holidays

Marrowfield Logistics recognises 8 public holidays per year, which are given
in addition to annual leave. Part-time employees receive public holidays pro
rata to their contracted hours, credited to their leave balance.

## Working on a public holiday

Depot operations continue on public holidays. An employee rostered to work
on a public holiday is paid at double time, as set out in the Overtime
policy, and also receives a day in lieu. The day in lieu must be taken within
3 months of the public holiday worked and is booked through the People Portal
in the same way as annual leave.

## Office closure at the end of the year

The head office closes from 27 to 31 December. Closure days that are not
public holidays are taken from annual leave: office-based employees must
reserve 3 days of their annual leave for the closure. Depot employees are
rostered as normal during the closure.

## Swapping a public holiday

Employees who observe a religious festival on a day that is not a public
holiday may swap a public holiday for that day with their line manager's
agreement. Requests must be made at least 4 weeks in advance.

## Public holidays during absence

A public holiday that falls during sick leave, maternity leave or adoption
leave is not lost; it is credited to your leave balance to take later. A
public holiday that falls during a period of annual leave is not deducted
from your annual leave.

## Where the dates are published

The dates of the 8 public holidays for the coming year are published on the
intranet each October, together with the depot rosters for those days.
